## Biography

### Another Life

Before pursuing computer science, I spent 10 years in retail management. I ran large-scale retail stores making upwards of $7M annually, managing up to 100 employees, with duties including operations, logistics, people management, and customer service.

At the other end of the spectrum, I ran small businesses such as gyms, franchise locations, and mall stores. Between the two, I learned how to increase productivity and roll out new projects while maintaining the personal connection that inspires a team to work for you and clients to trust you. I take pride in seeing my name on Google and Yelp reviews everywhere I've worked, knowing that I protected businesses from operational loss while growing the customer base through genuine customer-focused service.

This experience gave me communication, mediation, and conflict-resolution skills that benefit any team or company I work with. Even when I am not in a leadership position, I advocate for hearing every voice in the room, making sure everyone's position is understood and preventing miscommunication from hurting the team or project.

### The Engineer I Became

As I moved from learning software development into practicing it professionally, I became convinced that good design and clear systems are engineering concerns, not finishing touches. The most capable software in the world will not create value if people cannot understand or use it.

My focus became the practical relationship between product decisions, technical systems, and the people responsible for operating them. I care about interfaces that communicate clearly, systems that are easy to maintain, and engineering choices that hold up beyond the first release.

That perspective shapes how I evaluate technical and design decisions. I look for unnecessary complexity, unclear ownership, and friction that can be removed for users and teams alike.

### From Intern To Senior Engineer

During my computer science studies, I focused on machine learning, artificial intelligence, and front-end development. That foundation helped me move from internship into professional software engineering and then through the responsibilities of increasingly senior roles.

In five years, I moved from intern to Senior Software Engineer. I built breadth across front-end development, back-end services, cloud infrastructure, and delivery while keeping product outcomes and operational reality in view. Senior engineering, to me, is the ability to make sound technical decisions, explain tradeoffs clearly, and help a team solve the right problem rather than simply produce more code.

I currently work as a Senior Software Engineer for ClickBank, building full-stack solutions for its e-commerce and affiliate marketing platform. My work ranges from managing cloud infrastructure to designing and implementing full-stack solutions with React, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.
